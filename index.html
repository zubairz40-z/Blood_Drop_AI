Smart Features

An Intelligent System for Every Drop

BloodDrop AI uses multiple AI agents to make blood donation faster, smarter and safer.

AI Donor Matching

Our AI Manager coordinates agents to match patients with compatible donors instantly based on blood group and component type.

GPS-Based Search

Locate the nearest available donors to the hospital and suggest the fastest route for donation.

Donation Eligibility

Automatic eligibility checks — age, weight, last donation date and health status — before a donor is contacted.

Emergency Notifications

Critical requests instantly notify nearby donors with an accept / decline response flow.

Blood Component Support

Support for Whole Blood, Plasma, Platelets and Red Blood Cells with dedicated donor pools.

Analytics & Reports

Real-time statistics for admins — total donations, response times and the most requested blood groups.

How It Works

From Emergency to Life Saved

A fully automated multi-agent workflow from request to donation.

1. Request

A patient or hospital submits a blood request with blood group, component type and emergency level.

2. AI Coordination

The AI Manager activates the matching, eligibility and GPS agents to find the best donor.

3. Donation

The donor confirms, visits the hospital, and the donation history is automatically updated.
